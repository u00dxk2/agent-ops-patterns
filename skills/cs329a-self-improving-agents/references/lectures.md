# The nine lectures

Stanford CS329A "Self-Improving AI Agents", fall 2025 (course site: cs329a.stanford.edu). Instructors: Azalia Mirhoseini (Stanford CS; previously Google Brain / DeepMind, Anthropic) and Aakanksha Chowdhery (Stanford adjunct; Reflection AI). The videos carry "Stanford Online" in their titles and were posted in August 2026.

The videos are Stanford's; this file links them and summarizes them in our words. It does not contain transcripts. If you want the lecturer's exact wording on a point, watch the segment. If your operator has `yt-dlp` and is comfortable with YouTube's terms, `yt-dlp --skip-download --write-auto-subs --sub-langs en --sub-format vtt <url>` pulls the auto-captions; they are unpunctuated, speaker turns are unmarked, and student questions run into answers, so quote ideas from them, never sentences. The papers in `papers.md` are the primary source either way.
